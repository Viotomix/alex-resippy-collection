~110 min* | ?? Pieces

Dough:
- 125 g Butter
- 100 g Sugar
- 2 Eggs
- 300 g Flour
- 1 TSP Baking powder
- 1 PCK Vanilla sugar
- Some butter for the tray

Topping:
- 200 g Apricon jam
- 175 g Butter
- 130 g Sugar
- 1 PCK Vanilla sugar
- 400 g chopped Nuts (hazelnut, almonds or other)
- 200 g chocolate coating\*

![Nut Edges](https://www.einfachbacken.de/sites/einfachbacken.de/files/styles/700_530/public/2018-08/nussecken-3.jpg)

1. Preheat the oven at 180°C (Circulation: 160°C)\*
2. Thoroughly coat a baking tray (roughly 39x26x4 cm) in butter
3. Place all the dough ingredients into a large bowl and mix/knead it into a dough.
4. Roll the dough out into the tray and spread it until it covers the entire bottom evenly.
5. Coat the dough with the jam by spreading it with a spoon.
6. Melt butter, sugar and vanilla sugar in a pot. Then add the nuts and 4 TBSP of water and keep stirring while it cooks on low heat, until it forms a wet mass. Then spread it on the tray.
7. Bake the nut edges for ~25 mins, then let them cool for a moment. Cut the dough into squares and then triangles, **while the dough is still hot.** Then let them cool fully. (This may take an hour or two, so be patient.)
8. Roughly chop the chocolate coating and melt the chunks in a water bath. Pour the melted chocolate into a tall container.
9. Lay out a sheet or two of baking paper and dip the nut edges on both sides, finally placing them on the sheets.
10. Let them cool, preferably over night. Do not place them in the fridge.

**Addendum:**
- As mentioned before the nut edges should cool over night if possible, so the time estimate can differ *vastly* from the one given.
- If your oven heats up fast you may want to preheat the oven right before step 5.
