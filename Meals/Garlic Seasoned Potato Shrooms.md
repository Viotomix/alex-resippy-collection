~90min\* | 2-3 Portions\* / 40 Pieces

Dough:
- 440g potatoes
- 200g potato starch
- 150ml water

Seasoning:
- 14g garlic\*
- 1tbsp chili powder / paprika powder 
- 1tsp sugar
- 4tbsp soy sauce
- 1tbsp green onion
- 3tbsp cooking oil

Additionally:
- An empty glass bottle
- 4-5 ice cubes

![Potato shrooms](https://img.youtube.com/vi/-BYPCJNm5uo/maxresdefault.jpg)

1. Peel and chop the potatoes into bite sized pieces. Chop the garlic and green onion. Portion the rest of the ingredients if necessary.
2. Boil the potatoes and then mash them.
3. Thoroughly mix mashed potatoes and potato starch in a bowl. If the dough is still sticky, keep adding starch.
4. Slowly add water and keep kneading it until you get a soft, non cracking dough. **Be careful to add water slowly**, it's easy to add too much!\*
5. Break the dough down into little chunks and roll them into balls. Poke a bottle neck into the ball\* to form a little mushroom. *See image for reference.*
6. Add the potato shrooms into **boiling** water and then boil them for 2:30 mins. *As they finish boiling they will begin to float to the water surface.*
	-> In the meantime, boil the cooking oil.
7. Shock the potato shrooms by placing them into a bowl of ice water for a minute.
8. Drain the potato shrooms into a bowl and add the garlic, soy sauce, sugar, chili powder and green onion on top. Pour the boiling oil over it and mix thoroughly until the shrooms are coated.

**Addendum:**
- If you do not like the taste of garlic, try a different sauce. The shrooms should work well with a variety of different seasoning.
- If you do not *love* the taste of garlic **DO NOT PRESS IT**. While the original recipe calls for chopped over ground for taste, pressing the garlic is over the top. Simply chop it instead.
- 3 portions is a generous estimate by the original recipe. Yes the shrooms are filling, but if you'd like to make sure 3 hungry people are fed *properly* I'd recommend making a little bit more.
- This recipe does not necessarily need to take 90mins. That was simply the time it took 2 inexperienced cooks to make it the very first time. With more experience, better feel for the process and less fumbling this recipe could be made within an hour.
- At some point the bottle neck got so gunked up with potato, one of the shroom stems broke off and stay in the bottle. To avoid this wash off the bottle neck every couple pieces and dust it in starch to help it not stick.
- If you add too much water, use more starch to even it out. Don't overdo it or you'll be stuck in an endless loop or adding more.
