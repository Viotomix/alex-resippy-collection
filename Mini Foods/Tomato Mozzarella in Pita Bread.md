5-10 mins prep | 5 mins per pita | 1 Portion*

- 1 pita bread
- ~60g shredded mozzarella*
- 4 cherry tomatoes
- > 1 small onion*

1. Preheat a pan with a bit of oil or butter at medium heat.
2. Dice the onion and throw it into the pan. Fry until clear.
	-> In the meantime dice the tomatoes
3. Mix tomatoes, onion and mozzarella, salt and pepper by feel and mix thoroughly.
4.  Slice a thin piece off the top of the pita and make sure there's usable space inside the pita. Do this carefully to prevent the edges of the pita from tearing. Fill the pita with the mix and place it in the pan.
5. Fry both sides until the mozzarella has melted and both sides are as brown as you'd like them to be.

**Addendum:**
- I would recommend making at least 2 or 3 of these. The onion measures out better that way.
- The mozzarella measurement is estimated, we just used however much could fit into the pita, without it falling apart.
- The onion can be left out just fine and the recipe still works.
- Best eaten by hand, with a napkin
