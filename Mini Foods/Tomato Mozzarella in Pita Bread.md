5-10 mins prep | 5 mins per pita | 1 Portion

- 1 pita bread
- ~100g shredded mozzarella*
- 4 cherry tomatoes

1. Preheat a pan with a bit of oil or butter at medium heat.
2. Slice a thin piece off the top of the pita and make sure there's usable space inside the pita. Do this carefully to prevent the edges of the pita from tearing.
3. Dice the tomatoes
4. Mix tomatoes and mozzarella, salt and pepper by feel and mix thoroughly.
5. Fill the pita with the mix and place it in the pan.
6. Fry both sides until the mozzarella has melted and both sides are as brown as you'd like them to be.

**Addendum:**
- The mozzarella measurement is estimated, we just used however much could fit into the pita, without it falling apart.
- Best eaten by hand, with a napkin
- We have yet to try it, but this could work *very* well with some **onion**. If you'd like to try this simmer the onions mildly before mixing them with the rest of the ingredients.
